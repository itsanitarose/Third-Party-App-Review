## Current Consent Rules

- Users can approve any app requesting delegated permissions with one click. 
- No admin review is required
- No alerts are sent

<br>

## Available Identity Controls

The identity system can:

- Require admin approval for some or all apps.
- Allow users to approve only low-risk permissions from verified publishers.
- Remove app access for specific users or everyone.
- End a user's active sign-in sessions.
- Send alerts when users approve apps.

<br>

## What the Permissions Mean

- **Read basic profile:** Access your name and email address.
- **Read mail:** Read emails in your mailbox.
- **Read calendar:** View your calendar and appointments.
- **Send mail:** Send emails on your behalf.
- **Offline access:** Maintain access without you actively using the app, until access expires or is revoked.
- **Delegated permissions:** The app acts on behalf of a user, within its granted permissions and the user's access rights.
- **Application permissions:** The app acts independently, within its granted permissions, without a user signing in or completing MFA. Admin approval is required.
- **Verified publisher:** The app publisher's identity has been verified. This does not guarantee the app is safe. An unverified publisher is not necessarily malicious.
