# 03. Application Access Decisions

I assessed each application against its business justification, permission scope, access governance and impact on business continuity.

| Application | Decision | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| DocuSharePro | Remove | An unverified app with no business owner, extensive permissions and a weekend consent spike suggests OAuth consent phishing, justifying immediate revocation despite potential business disruption. |
| ExportBot | Restrict | Nightly clinic reporting provides legitimate business value, justifying continued operation while addressing missing ownership, an unchanged secret and unrestricted access to all mailboxes. |
