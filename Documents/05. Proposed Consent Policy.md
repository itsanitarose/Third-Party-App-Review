# 05. Proposed Consent Policy

Employees may self-approve only low-risk delegated permissions for applications from verified publishers.

Unverified applications, higher-risk permissions and all application permissions require admin approval through a formal approval workflow, supported by documented business justification and an assigned business owner.

Every consent approval triggers an automated alert to IT.

MailMerge Plus remains operational following formal admin approval, with existing authorised Marketing users retaining access and new users following the approved access request process.
