# 04. DocuSharePro Response

Given the suspicious consent activity affecting 112 users, I would treat DocuSharePro as a suspected OAuth consent phishing incident and escalate to Security.

## Step 1: Containment

**Disable** DocuSharePro and **revoke its existing consent grants** for all 112 users.

- **Stops:** New consent approvals and token issuance.
- **Still exposed:** Previously issued access tokens may remain valid until expiry or invalidation. Refresh tokens granted through "stay signed in" (offline access) must also be revoked. Any data already accessed or modified remains an investigation concern.
- **Why first:** Remove the application's authorisation at the directory level before revoking user sessions. Otherwise, the application remains authorised and can obtain new tokens.
